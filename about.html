About PayMap

Track where it goes.

PayMap is a lightweight expense tracking application created to make everyday expense management simpler. The goal is to provide the essential tools for recording, organizing and exporting expenses without unnecessary complexity.

What PayMap does

  • Record an expense with a name, amount, date and category.
  • Organize spending into simple categories — Rent / Housing, Food, Transport, Utilities, Health, Leisure and Other.
  • Show your current total spending and most recent expenses.
  • Export your records to PDF or Excel.
  • Start a new expense period when you are done with the current one.

Local-first & private

PayMap stores your expenses on your device. It does not require an account, and it does not send your expense data to a server. Files you export are only shared when you choose to share them.
