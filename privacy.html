Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

PayMap is an expense-tracking application that helps users record, organize, calculate, and export their expenses.

We respect your privacy and have designed PayMap so that your expense records are stored locally on your device.

1. Information You Enter

PayMap allows you to enter information such as:

  • Expense names or descriptions
  • Expense amounts
  • Expense dates
  • Expense categories

This information is used to provide the application’s expense-tracking features.

2. Local Storage

PayMap stores your expense records locally on your device using local application storage.

Your expense records are not uploaded to a PayMap server and PayMap does not require you to create an account.

We do not have access to the expense records stored within your application.

Deleting the application or clearing its application data may permanently delete locally stored expense records.

3. Exporting Expenses

PayMap can generate expense reports in PDF and Excel formats.

These files are generated locally on your device.

When you choose to share an exported file, Android’s sharing system may allow you to send the file to another application or person. Once you share a file outside PayMap, its handling is governed by the privacy practices of the application or service receiving it.

PayMap does not control information after you choose to share an exported file.

4. Data Collection and Sharing

PayMap does not sell, rent, or share your expense records with third parties.

The current version does not use:

  • User accounts
  • Cloud synchronization
  • Advertising services
  • Analytics services
  • Firebase services
  • A PayMap backend or cloud database

PayMap does not collect your expense records for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

5. Internet Access

PayMap’s core expense-tracking functionality is designed to work locally on your device.

The application does not require an online account or a PayMap server to create, manage, calculate, or export your expenses.

Certain third-party software libraries used by the application may have their own technical network functionality. These libraries do not give PayMap access to your expense records.

6. Financial Information

PayMap is an expense-tracking application.

It does not:

  • Connect to bank accounts
  • Process payments
  • Store banking credentials
  • Access credit/debit card accounts
  • Provide investment advice
  • Provide financial advice

Users should avoid entering passwords, banking credentials, or other highly sensitive information into expense descriptions.

7. Children’s Privacy

PayMap is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Security

Because expense records are stored locally on your device, you are responsible for protecting your device.

We recommend using a device screen lock and keeping your operating system up to date.

9. Third-Party Applications

PayMap may use Android’s standard sharing functionality when you choose to share an exported PDF or Excel file.

Once information or an exported file is transferred to another application, that application’s own privacy policy applies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if PayMap’s functionality or data practices change.

The “Last updated” date at the beginning of this policy will be updated when changes are made.
